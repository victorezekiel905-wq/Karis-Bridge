Learning at Karisbridge

Each stage builds on the one before, so children move up with confidence. Here is what learning looks like in our creche, nursery and primary classes.

On this page: Creche Nursery Primary Beyond the classroom Summer School

Babies & toddlers

Creche

A calm, loving first step away from home.

Our creche is clean, bright and unhurried. Caregivers follow each child’s own rhythm for feeding, rest and play, and fill the day with songs, stories and things to touch and explore.

What the day includes

  • Warm, attentive caregivers and familiar daily routines
  • Sensory play, music and movement
  • First words, rhymes and picture books
  • Early sharing and playing alongside friends
  • Safe, clean spaces for rest, meals and care
Enquire about the creche

Early years

Nursery

Where play becomes real learning.

Nursery children learn by doing. Counting sticks and beads make numbers something to hold, stories build vocabulary, and projects, like becoming the roots, leaves and flowers of a plant, turn big ideas into moments they remember.

What the day includes

  • Letter sounds, early reading and mark-making
  • Number work with counting sticks, beads and shapes
  • Science and nature through play and observation
  • Art, craft, songs, role-play and performance
  • Growing independence, good manners and friendship
Enquire about nursery

Primary years

Primary

Strong foundations and a wider view.

Primary pupils build secure skills in English, mathematics and science, then put them to work through ICT, projects and presentations. We want them to be articulate, curious and proud of what they produce.

What the day includes

  • English, Mathematics and Science
  • ICT and block-based coding in the computer lab
  • Social studies, culture and world awareness
  • Projects, presentations and public speaking
  • Responsibility, leadership and good character
Enquire about primary

How we teach

The same principles in every class

Whatever the age or the subject, these are the habits you will see in a Karisbridge classroom.

  • Hands-on

    Children learn by making, testing and exploring, not only by listening.

  • Known by name

    Teachers know each child’s strengths, worries and next steps.

  • Partners with parents

    We keep families close to their child’s learning and progress.

  • Progress celebrated

    Award days and performances give every child a moment to shine.

Beyond the classroom

Something new every week

Activities are woven through the week, so every child gets to try new things and find out what they enjoy.

  • Coding & ICT

    Block-based coding and everyday computer skills in our lab.

  • Cooking

    Measuring, mixing and tasting, with lessons in hygiene and patience.

  • Science

    Experiments with colour, water, plants and materials.

  • Art & craft

    Drawing, painting, colouring and weaving.

  • Hands-on maths

    Beads, sticks and trays that make numbers something to hold.

  • Culture & performance

    Costumes, songs and stories from around the world, performed on stage.

  • Outdoor play

    Water play, nature and time on the playground and turf.

  • Health & wellbeing

    Talks and visits that teach children to look after themselves.

KBS Summer School

The long vacation, well spent

During the long vacation, KBS Summer School keeps curiosity going with hands-on activities, creativity and play. It is a happy way for children to keep learning while school is out.
