Welcome to Karisbridge

A joyful school with big dreams for every child.

At Karisbridge, children are known by name, taught with care and encouraged to be their very best, in the classroom, on the stage and in the way they treat one another.

Karis is the Greek word for grace. A bridge carries you somewhere new. Together they describe what we set out to be: a warm, gracious school that carries children confidently from home into learning, and from learning into the wider world.

Why families choose us

The Karisbridge difference.

Six promises you will notice from the moment you walk through our gate.

  • Character first

    Kindness, respect and good manners are taught, modelled and celebrated every day.

  • Holistic learning

    Strong foundations in reading, number and science, alongside art, music and play.

  • Safe and caring

    A calm, gated campus in Kajola Estate, with attentive adults who know every child.

  • STEM and ICT

    Coding, circuits and hands-on experiments that make children ask why.

  • Creative arts

    Ballet, martial arts, music, craft and performances that build real confidence.

  • Parents as partners

    Open, friendly communication and celebrations that families share with us.
