Admissions

We welcome enquiries for the creche, nursery and primary school. Here is how joining works, what to prepare and how to reach us.

How to join

Five simple steps

Starting at a new school is a big moment for a family. We keep the process clear and personal, so you always know what comes next.

  1. 1

    Enquire

    Call, WhatsApp us or use the form below, and tell us a little about your child.

  2. 2

    Visit

    Come for a tour, meet the team and see the classrooms for yourself.

  3. 3

    Meet

    A short, friendly meeting helps us place your child in the right class.

  4. 4

    Register

    Complete the registration form and hand in the documents listed below.

  5. 5

    Start

    We help your child settle in gently, and keep in touch as they do.

What to bring

For registration

  • A completed registration form
  • A copy of your child’s birth certificate
  • Recent passport photographs of your child
  • Your child’s immunisation record
  • The most recent school report (for primary)

Our admissions team will confirm the full list for your child’s class.

Questions

Questions parents often ask

Can’t find your answer? Contact us

Which classes do you offer?

We have a creche for babies and toddlers, nursery classes for the early years, and a primary school. Our admissions team will help you find the right class for your child’s age.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, and we encourage it. Book a visit by phone, WhatsApp or the enquiry form above and we will arrange a time that suits you.

Is there an assessment for new pupils?

For nursery and primary we arrange a short, relaxed meeting with your child so we can understand where they are and place them in the right class. It is not a stressful test.

Can my child join during the school year?

Where there is space, yes. Please get in touch to ask about availability in your child’s class.

How do I find out about fees?

Our fee schedule is available on request. Ask us by phone or WhatsApp, or during your visit, and we will talk you through it.

Where exactly is the school?

We are at 1st Avenue, Kajola Estate Phase 1, off the Lekki–Epe Expressway in Ibeju-Lekki, Lagos. Look for the ocean mural at the entrance.
