Graduation 2026

Around the world, on one stage.

At our 2nd Graduation and Award Ceremony, pupils represented every continent in costume and song. The nursery class became the roots and flowers of a plant, our ballerinas and martial artists took the stage, and our youngest proudly held up the shapes they had learned.

Summer 2026

Little chefs and young innovators.

KBS Summer School kept curiosity going all vacation. Our young chefs rolled up their sleeves and made moi-moi, and our young innovators built circuits and simple machines outdoors.

September 2026

Champions back for a new year.

The new session began with sunflower frames, First Day sunglasses and a dancing welcome from our teachers. We could not be happier to have our Champions back.
