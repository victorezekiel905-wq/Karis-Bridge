Celebrations, activities and the everyday moments in between. Here is a look at what goes on at Karisbridge.

Graduation & Award Ceremony

Around the world, on one stage

For our 2nd Graduation and Award Ceremony, pupils represented every continent in song and costume. The nursery class became the roots, leaves and flowers of a plant, and our toddlers proudly held up the shapes they had learned. It was a lovely afternoon, and families still talk about it.
