Learning thatgrows withyour child.

Each stage builds on the one before, so children move up with confidence. Here is what learning looks like in our creche, nursery and primary classes.

CrecheNurseryPrimaryEnrichmentSummer School
Hands-on, every day

01 · Babies & toddlers

Creche.

A calm, loving first step away from home.

Our creche is clean, bright and unhurried. Caregivers follow each child’s own rhythm for feeding, rest and play, and fill the day with songs, stories and things to touch and explore.

  • Warm, attentive caregivers and familiar routines
  • Sensory play, music and movement
  • First words, rhymes and picture books
  • Safe, clean spaces for rest, meals and care
Enquire about Creche

02 · Early years

Nursery.

Where play becomes real learning.

Nursery children learn by doing. Counting sticks and beads make numbers real, stories build vocabulary, and projects like becoming the roots and flowers of a plant turn big ideas into moments they remember.

  • Letter sounds, early reading and mark-making
  • Hands-on number work with real objects
  • Science and nature through play
  • Art, songs, role-play and performance
Enquire about Nursery

03 · Primary years

Primary.

Strong foundations and a wider view.

Primary pupils build secure skills in English, mathematics and science, then put them to work through ICT, STEM projects and presentations. We want them articulate, curious and proud of what they produce.

  • English, Mathematics and Science
  • ICT, coding and STEM projects
  • Social studies, culture and world awareness
  • Presentations, leadership and good character
Enquire about Primary

How we teach

Four habits in every classroom.

Whatever the age or subject, these are the principles you will see at work at Karisbridge.

  • Hands-on

    Children learn by making, testing and exploring, not only by listening.

  • Known by name

    Teachers know each child’s strengths, worries and next steps.

  • Partners with parents

    We keep families close to their child’s learning and progress.

  • Progress celebrated

    Award days and performances give every child a moment to shine.

KBS Summer School

The long vacation, well spent.

In summer 2026 our young chefs made moi-moi from scratch and our young innovators stepped into the world of STEM.

KBS Summer School keeps curiosity switched on while school is out, with cooking, science, art and plenty of play. It is open to Karisbridge pupils and friends from the community.
