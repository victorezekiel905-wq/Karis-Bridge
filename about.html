Grace thatcarries childrenforward.

Karisbridge Schools gives children a happy, all-round start: loving care in the creche, learning through play in nursery and strong foundations in primary.

Creche · Nursery · Primary

Our name

Two words, one promise.

Our name describes the kind of school we set out to be, and the journey we take with every child.

Karis

From the Greek χάρις, meaning grace

Grace is kindness given freely. It is the teacher who kneels to a child’s eye level, and the warmth that makes a new classroom feel like home.

Bridge

A way across to somewhere new

A bridge carries you forward: from home to school, from curiosity to confidence, and from Lagos out into the wider world.

Our vision

To shape global citizens through holistic learning.

Our mission

To give every child a holistic education that builds vital skills and excellent character, in a safe, joyful and loving place.

Our values

What we care about.

  • Grace

    We lead with kindness and patience, and give every child room to grow at their own pace.

  • Excellence

    We expect a lot of our pupils, and we help each one of them get there, step by step.

  • Curiosity

    Questions are welcome here. We test ideas, try things out and enjoy finding out.

  • Kindness

    We look out for one another in the classroom, in the playground and at home.

  • Confidence

    From class presentations to the graduation stage, children learn their voice matters.

  • Respect

    We are proud of our Nigerian roots and curious about cultures from every continent.

Our approach

Head, heart and hands.

Holistic education means educating the whole child. Every lesson and celebration is designed to grow all three, together.

Head

Minds that think

Strong foundations in literacy, numeracy and science, taught with clarity so children understand, not just remember.

Heart

Hearts that care

Good manners, empathy, honesty and courage are modelled by our teachers and celebrated in our children.

Hands

Hands that make

Cooking, coding, crafting and performing: practical, joyful work that turns lessons into lasting skills.

Our teachers

A team that loves what they do.

“Teachers ready. Parents ready. Let’s go!” That was the mood on the first day of the 2026/2027 session, and it is the energy our children meet every morning.

Our caregivers and teachers are warm, capable and easy to talk to. They know each child by name, notice the small wins and share them with you.

Join our team

Painted on our wall

“We don’t bully. We love.”

One of the first things visitors notice, and a rule our pupils are proud to keep.
