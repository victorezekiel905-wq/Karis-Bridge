Home/About

A school built on grace and good character

Karisbridge Schools gives young children a happy, all-round start: loving care in the creche, learning through play in nursery and strong foundations in primary.

Our name

What “Karisbridge” means

Two ideas sit inside our name. Together they describe the kind of school we set out to be.

Karis

From the Greek χάρις, meaning grace

Grace is kindness given freely. It is the teacher who kneels down to a child’s eye level, and the warmth that makes a new classroom feel like home.

Bridge

A way across to somewhere new

A bridge carries you forward: from home to school, from curiosity to confidence, and from Lagos out into the wider world. It has to be strong enough to cross boldly.

Our vision

To shape global citizens through holistic learning.

Our mission

To give every child a holistic education that builds vital skills and excellent character, in a safe, joyful and loving environment.

Our values

What we care about

These values shape how we teach, how we speak to one another and what we choose to celebrate.

  • Grace

    We lead with kindness and patience, and give every child room to grow at their own pace.

  • Excellence

    We expect a lot of our pupils, and we help each one of them get there, a step at a time.

  • Curiosity

    Questions are welcome here. We test ideas, try things out and enjoy finding out.

  • Kindness

    We look out for one another, in the classroom, in the playground and at home.

  • Confidence

    From class presentations to the graduation stage, children learn that their voice matters.

  • Respect

    We are proud of our Nigerian roots and curious about cultures from every part of the world.

Painted on our wall

“We don’t bully. We love.”

It is one of the first things visitors notice, and a rule our pupils are proud to keep. Kindness is taught, expected and celebrated at Karisbridge.

Families

Parents are our partners

Children do best when home and school pull in the same direction. Our teachers know each child well, notice the small wins and share them with you.

  • Teachers you can talk to, and a school office that is easy to reach
  • Regular updates on your child’s learning, wellbeing and next steps
  • Graduations, award ceremonies and special days to share with us
